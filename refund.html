Refund & Rework Policy

Change of mind

Because Make You Stop creates personalised creative work for each order, change-of-mind refunds are not available once production has started.

If something is wrong with the delivered work

If the delivered work contains an error or materially does not match the confirmed brief, submit an Order Review Request. We will review the order and, where required, correct the issue or provide another appropriate remedy.

Extra changes or a new direction

Changes that go beyond the confirmed brief — including a new creative direction, additional copy, additional versions, new assets, new claims or extra rework — may require a new order or an additional fee. Any additional cost will be confirmed before extra work starts.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer law.

Questions or review requests

Use the Order Support section on the Make You Stop website or contact hello@makeyoustop.com.
