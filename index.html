FAQ

Choice without the complicated agency process.

Do I have to buy a bundle?

No. Start with one item and add more later.

Can I use my own images or videos?

Yes. Supplied assets are part of the direction we are building, with customer control over how they are used.

Can approved assets be reused for my future jobs?

Yes, with permission, approved assets can be retained for future work for the same customer or brand.

Do you guarantee views, followers or sales?

No. Make You Stop improves the creative thinking and execution; platform outcomes are not guaranteed.

What if something is wrong with my order?

If we’ve made an error or the delivered item doesn’t match the confirmed brief, contact us and we’ll review it. Extra creative changes or a new direction may require a new order or quote.
