## Etsy description draft

**Plan stronger hooks before you post.**

The Creator Hook Planner & Content Worksheet is a practical printable planning pack for creators, small businesses and personal brands who want a clearer way to plan the opening of short-form content.

Use the worksheets to capture your content idea, decide what the viewer should feel, create an unanswered question, plan the first visual, rewrite weaker openings and track which hooks you actually use.

### You receive
- 8-page Creator Hook Planner
- A4 PDF
- US Letter PDF
- Instant digital download

### Useful for
TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and other short-form social content.

This is a digital planning template. No physical item will be shipped. It does not include personalised hooks or done-for-you content.
