## Product strategy
Use Etsy for eligible ready-made seller-designed digital products such as:
- planners
- worksheets
- templates
- printable creative tools
- visual assets

Do not use Etsy for:
- functional guides/instructions that do not fit Etsy's current marketplace rules
- prohibited prompt bundles
- low-quality or automatically published products/listings
- current custom creative service lanes as ordinary services

## Relationship to MakeYouStop.com
Etsy is an acquisition channel. MakeYouStop.com remains the main branded store and the home for custom generation, personalised products, direct upgrades and ready-made guides that may not fit Etsy marketplace policy.
